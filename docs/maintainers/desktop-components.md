## Conditional qualifications and review owners

The maintenance/release owner reviews these at the integrated 0.0.21 freeze, and whenever the named dependency or owner changes:

- **Fetch bootstrap:** retain both main and catalogue-worker entry points until an inspected, supported Electron supplies the fix in each executing context. The original 0.0.20 main-process crash reproduction is historical evidence, not a new worker-fault or installed-candidate pass.
- **Embedded Undici TLS:** [GHSA-qrxm-fqhp-jwvm](https://github.com/nodejs/undici/security/advisories/GHSA-qrxm-fqhp-jwvm) remains conditional on the mapped Electron/Node/BoringSSL default connector. The earlier source-inferred non-applicability is not a generic TLS guarantee. [GHSA-2m5v-pch6-74hx](https://github.com/nodejs/undici/security/advisories/GHSA-2m5v-pch6-74hx) requires function-valued custom `tls` options, not established in this app. Revisit on connector, custom Agent/TLS or Electron changes; do not treat the global API replacement as patching every internal embedded consumer.
- **Build-tool Undici 6.29.1:** node-gyp's separate copy includes the October correction, but [6.x is past its stated support end](https://github.com/nodejs/undici/blob/v7.30.1/README.md#long-term-support). Packaging owner checks node-gyp's supported replacement at the next toolchain update. This is not the desktop fetch implementation.
- **Scoped overrides:** maintenance owner removes each when Miniflare adopts a fixed version. Re-evaluate at each Miniflare bump; its local HTTP/R2 tests remain acceptance requirements.
- **Packaging cache advisory, disputed:** the lockfile resolves `@electron/get > got > cacheable-request > http-cache-semantics@4.3.0`, which the advisory database excludes, so audit no longer reports it. The [published 4.3.0 source](https://registry.npmjs.org/http-cache-semantics/4.3.0) leaves the cited `max-stale` path unchanged. The [maintainer declined the report](https://github.com/kornelski/http-cache-semantics/issues/56#issuecomment-5975759591), citing RFC 9111's cache rules, and closed it as not planned. The [database dispute](https://github.com/github/advisory-database/issues/10139) remains a separate decision; do not describe 4.3.0 as its fix. No shared authenticated cache was established in our packaging path. Packaging owner should resolve this qualification through the upstream dispute outcome or demonstrated non-use at release freeze or the next `@electron/get`/got update. This is not a waiver or a demonstrated desktop vulnerability.
- **Release-age exceptions:** updater 6.8.12 and helper 9.7.1 were selected from the maintainer's stable `v26` distribution and inspected release notes before 24 hours had elapsed. Exact-version exceptions preserve the general age policy. Maintenance owner can remove them after 27 October 2026 12:35 UTC. Existing TanStack/Workers exceptions remain unchanged and return to their owners on the next corresponding bump.
- **Signing/licences:** Wout retains the unresolved Microsoft Direct3D, WinShell, SpiderBanner and corresponding-source qualifications recorded in [open questions](licences.md#open-questions). Signing owner retains the single-shot notarization/stapling patch until its upstream owner changes. None is waived by a successful audit or build.

`pnpm audit --json` is an input, not product applicability proof. This selection removes the affected Happy DOM, Miniflare Undici and Sharp versions, and audit reports none. The packaging-cache advisory remains open; neither an audit result nor a source pin declares every shipped binary safe.
