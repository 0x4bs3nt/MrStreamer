# Product-use and reliability statistics

## How to get to it

Open Settings > General > Privacy. Share basic usage statistics is on by default. Turn it off with the checkbox; the helper must match the delivered catalogue in `docs/telemetry.md`, and Privacy policy opens the canonical policy in the system browser. No first-use notice or upgrade prompt is shown. Settings > About retains its Privacy link.

## Driving the built app

After `pnpm build`, run `xvfb-run -a node apps/desktop/test/e2e/analytics-app.ts` on headless Linux. The helper owns an isolated profile, loopback capture endpoint and debugging ports. It drives the Settings checkbox with pointer input, observes ordinary preferences and actual capture payloads, restarts the same isolated profile, and retains screenshots, payloads, instance identity and cleanup proof under `.local/verification/analytics-*`. It uses fake provider/TMDB boundaries and a loopback release feed, with automatic updates off. Product mutations use pointer/keyboard input. Named pointer targets are resolved again after scrolling and hover, require settled geometry and a matching hit, and retain the actual mousedown target in `proof.json`. It does not advance the clock; UTC rollover of `app_active_day` is covered by the focused owner tests in `apps/desktop/test/analytics.test.ts`. Production transport is impossible for this isolated profile, even if a release bundle contains production configuration.

Require a checked default and exactly one installation_observed plus one app_active_day. Every captured raw body must equal its schema-reconstructed allowlist; the proof records the exact property-key set per event name. Drive EPG expansion, a committed channel favourite and a nonempty global search, each with its own wire event, then open a continuous fake broadcast from the search. Require one live stream_started, at least 34 seconds of real playback, a Home preview of at least 12 seconds with no start or stop, and a return to Watch without another start. Opening a provider VOD ends the live run: its one stream_stopped must report at least 32 seconds and no more than the observed wall time minus the preview, plus 3 seconds for the sink's view of the start. VOD pause, a paused seek and resume keep one start; Watch offline starts a second VOD run and stops the first. Drive completed, cancelled and failed downloads (download_ended success/interrupted/failed), a failed title open with exactly one stream_error and no start, all ten fixed screen entries, the autoplay-next preference (settings_changed with setting_key only), successful and failed manual update checks, an offered update shown and dismissed through Not now, and playlist_added/playlist_refreshed from the fixture login. Force a crash of only the owned renderer, require exactly one renderer_exited, and reload it. No app_summary_day may be sent and no event may arrive twice. Then require a visibly unchecked control after disabling, no further captures after disabling or restarting, and the same installation identity with no duplicate first-seen/day capture after enabling again.

A second profile is seeded with a format-one analytics.json holding daily counts and a queued app_summary_day beside a queued installation_observed. The app must send that installation_observed unchanged, under the seeded identity, never send the summary, and rewrite the file as format two without counts. A third fresh profile faces a refusing loopback capture server: it must retry a refused event with an identical body, hold actual pending events, then clear the persisted queue through Settings opt-out and make no further requests after restart. Unpackaged build attribution is `unknown`. The helper also accepts a packaged executable and optional app arguments, retains its hash/renderer identity and checks its actual version and Stable/Nightly channel. Native installed acceptance stays separate. Inspect screenshots and actual local payloads. Pointer and keys perform mutations; the decoded wire capture establishes results. All five translated controls are checked by focused renderer tests; native platform acceptance remains separate.

The fixture uses a checkout-owned ffmpeg stream of the existing MPEG-TS recording; aborting the owned session ends it. No production broadcast is used for this telemetry acceptance.

## Limits

`docs/privacy.md` holds the published policy, website analytics and attributed provider retention terms. Its version-qualified desktop 0.0.15 and 0.0.16 event list and the Settings note describe the app's events; before release, check them against `docs/telemetry.md`. Loopback payloads prove the integration contract; live ingestion, dashboard counts and server deletion deadlines remain unverified. `apps/desktop/test/analytics.test.ts` checks the owner's public contract: exact local HTTP payloads per event, consent generations for transactions and operations, update-channel stamping, quit flushing of the current run's stop, rate limiting and the 64-event outbox, retries under the same UUID, format-one migration that discards queued summaries, and opt-out during startup. Its playback cases cover preview eligibility, incident deduplication and recovery, stale bindings and sequences, renderer loss with remote continuation, leases and suspend, consent ABA without historical starts or stops, receiver load failures before and after Output's observer, replay controls and normal-admission fallback. `analytics-open.test.ts` checks that only a current open's own failure reports a fixed code. Receiver terminal ends are checked at the owner (with and without a renderer, against replaced loads, output moves, older runs and opt-out), through a fake Cast receiver in `output.test.ts` (played to its end, Stop, stopped on the TV, release and a broken channel stream) and in the renderer controllers in `renderer/receiver.test.ts`. Public transaction tests check shared refresh, source no-ops, favourite variants/receipts and reorder exclusion, effective preference no-ops and current-target update dismissal. A fake Cast receiver drives generation-guarded main Output observations; it does not establish real AirPlay/Cast hardware acceptance. Renderer contracts check unpaused progress and seek/wait/pause observations without transmitting media positions. Exact-candidate installed native checks stay on the release card. Real-provider playback uses the separate authorized acceptance workflow. Abrupt termination without quit can lose an unflushed stream_stopped and is not exercised.

Actual renderer exits must be observed from main; main crashes and power loss cannot be claimed from these events.

## Bounded desktop playback diagnostics from 0.0.16

The existing failed-title-open step must retain exactly one `stream_error` and no new start, with typed `deliver` / `provider_unavailable` context in its actual loopback HTTP body. The maintained helper records that event in `observations.failedOpenDiagnostic`. The common allowlist comparison still applies to every raw body. Public analytics/open/renderer contracts cover strict literal validation, future optional diagnostic loading without losing consent/identity/legacy work, repeated/recovered/stale incidents, receiver moves, preview and opt-out exclusion. Playback service fixtures cover missing/failed tools, failed probing, malformed probe output, conversion failure, decode rejection and provider/network evidence without reading raw messages for a codec cause. An actual 0.0.15 loader fixture is retained in the unit's local delivery evidence; the old loader deletes only additive diagnostic properties. Native installed proof and post-release production breakdown remain release-owner checks.
