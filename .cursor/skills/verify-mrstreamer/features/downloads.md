# Downloads

## Sub-features

Download a movie, a whole series or a single episode from its details, a persistent queue with one transfer at a time, the top bar's notice of how it stands, the Downloads page with queue and copies, offline playback of a copy in the title player, playback of the same subscription taking the connection, cancel, retry, delete, interrupted and replaced files, and copies kept after their subscription is removed.

## How to get to it (user POV)

A movie's details have **Download** beside Play and Save; a series' **Download** menu previews all available seasons, including Specials, its selected Play version and eligible/already-downloaded/already-queued counts. Confirm **Download … episodes** to append one file per logical episode in `seriesEpisodeOrder`, using the same `episodeFileVersion` rule as the row, with a resumed file only while listed. Complete, transferring, waiting and queued files are skipped; failed files use the existing Retry. Partial Add failures retain successful additions and name failed episodes with Retry. Changing the selected episode files, including marking a resumed episode watched or refreshing the default language, hides the old result and Retry. A late answer cannot restore that obsolete feedback; copies already queued remain in Downloads. The menu aggregates the existing statuses and offers Retry failed, Cancel the rest and Open Downloads. A version switch or unmount stops further additions after any submitted call finishes. Each episode row has a fixed icon target (`Download S1 E2`), status first in the facts line and a 3px progress bar under its still. Its cancel, retry, missing and offline icons keep the overflow control aligned. Movie and series pills reserve their widest state label in the current language, at least 200px, so Save keeps its place in every state. The control follows the download: Queued, a percentage, Waiting for playback, an error with Retry, then **Watch offline** (a movie's arrow beside it holds **Delete download**; an episode's reads **Downloaded**). **Downloads**, the last page in the top bar, lists the queue, then the copies on this computer with **Watch offline** and **Delete** (pressed twice). Below the width where the visible page names fit it is the download icon beside Search, with the same name. While a download transfers, waits or is queued, the top bar's notice beside Search says so on every page but Downloads with its name in the bar: the arrow and a floored percentage, the bytes so far when the size is unknown (Starting before the first), **Waiting** or **Queued**, muted; **Download stopped** once a failure of a saved subscription is all that is left. Its name is "Downloads, <what it says>". Where the bar folds, it is the folded Downloads button, marked on its page. Where the other pages' names, or Back, have no room beside its words, it keeps its arrow alone and says them on hover and focus. Pressing it, with the pointer, Enter or Space, opens Downloads; over a playing title it leaves the title as Back does first, and over Watch or a title the player's keys leave Enter and Space on it to the button. With no subscription saved, Connect shows **Downloads** top right while a copy exists; that page has only Downloads and **Add subscription**.

## Driving it with Electron CDP

Run `xvfb-run -a pnpm verify:desktop downloads` after `pnpm build` (no Xvfb on macOS or Windows). It starts the app on an English system; `pnpm verify:desktop downloads fr-FR`, or another interface language, starts it on a system in that one, so System default shows it, and looks for each control and message in that language. The scenario in `scripts/downloads.ts` starts the built app three times on one profile, with the fake provider, fake TMDB and a loopback picture server, and drives it with pointer and key input:

1. Connect; download the two-sound-track movie from its details and S1 E2 of the Formats series from its row, until **Watch offline** and **Downloaded** show.
2. Hold the long-subtitles movie's file at 40 kB, download it, then Play the index-at-the-end movie of the same subscription. Require a moving picture, `waiting` for the held download, and at most one provider file at once; after leaving, require every download complete. Then hold the broadcast-recording movie's file, download it, play a channel from Search and press Escape for Home's muted preview: the download waits, and its details opened from Search read Waiting for playback · Cancel, which cancels it.
3. Download the missing-file movie, Retry it from the details (a new file request), cancel a held download and delete the failed one on the Downloads page.
4. Quit, stop the provider, TMDB and pictures, start again. On Downloads, play the movie offline, skip forward, choose the other sound track, leave; play the long movie, choose its French text track, skip to 40 s, require cues and a caption on screen, leave and, as soon as main's list keeps where it was left, press Watch offline: it resumes near there though the row may not show it yet (`resume.shownAtResume`), and the row shows it after.
   Along steps 2 and 3 the top bar's notice is read and pressed: a percentage while the long movie transfers; Waiting over the playing title, where Tab and Space on it leave the title, saving where it got to, and open Downloads with no details over it; Waiting over Watch, where Tab and Enter on it close Watch over Downloads and the download takes the connection on; Waiting behind Home's preview; beside Search and Downloads among the pages at the window's width, Enter on it opening Downloads, which then shows no notice; the folded Downloads button marked on its page, and Space on it from Movies; Download stopped with the failed download alone left, pressed back to Downloads; nothing once only copies are left. Over the details, with Back in the pages' place, the bar is narrowed 20 CSS px at a time until Download stopped keeps its arrow alone, and the held movie's percentage must show there as it starts, nothing resized. At the narrowest width where the folded Downloads says the percentage, Tab to it and cancel the transfer from its row with the pointer: Download stopped goes to its arrow with the same button in focus; on Movies, Tab to it again, point at it for the tooltip, then the window's own width (words back) and the narrow one (arrow again) keep that focus, and Enter opens Downloads. Every page draws its own bar, so opening another page starts the keyboard over. While it transfers, waits and stopped, the bar is laid out at an emulated 960 and 760 CSS px (the window's least width is 960), and Download stopped over Settings at 960 and 520: each read fails the run when a page's name, Back or a control is cut. A MutationObserver records every word the visible notice said as `notice.said`, empty while the arrow stands alone.
5. Remove the subscription in Settings, quit, start again. Connect shows Downloads; the page lists all copies with "Subscription removed, copy kept", local artwork loaded and progress. Play the episode and resume the long movie. Remove the two-track movie's file from the run's profile, Watch it offline, require **Download missing**, press Back and require its row to say the file is gone, with Delete and no Watch offline, before main's list is read. **Add subscription** returns to the form.

`network-hook.cjs` is loaded into main with `NODE_OPTIONS=--require` for this scenario only: it writes every outbound TCP connection and UDP send to `main-network.jsonl` and answers the image CDN hosts from the run's picture server. The window's requests come from CDP `Network.requestWillBeSent`. Each local playback window must show no main connection and no window request except the loopback proxy's `/title/` and `/source/` routes, `mrstreamer:`, `data:`, `blob:` and `file:`. Proof is in `.local/verification/downloads-<unique>/`, or `downloads-<locale>-<unique>/` in another language: `proof.json` (its `locale`, actions, observations including `notice` (each bar read with the pages' names, `room` and `needs` in CSS px, whether a name or control is `clipped` or `overflows` the bar, and the Downloads controls beside Search; `fromTitle` with the progress main saved, `fromWatch`, and `<state>AtWidths`), `preemption`, `waitingBehindPreview`, `seek`, `soundTracks`, `embeddedSubtitleCues`, `captionShown`, `resume`, `standalone`, `offlinePlayback`, `playbackWithoutSubscription`, `missingCopy`, each start's checked `starts` and a `mainNetwork` summary), screenshots with accessibility trees, `main-network.jsonl` and `electron.log`.

## Gotchas

Fixture names come back from TMDB as "Original 100000" and similar: the scenario finds rows by the download id main lists, never by provider names. The 20-second fixture is all credits by the 30-second rule, so resume is proved on the 150-second one. The first track of the long-subtitles fixture is pictures, drawn from a metadata track: text cues need its French track. Home's muted preview is playback too, so a download of that subscription waits while Home shows it. The proof uses fixtures and a development build on Linux; disk-full and folder failures are injected at the disk boundary in the service tests, not provoked on a real disk. Native macOS and Windows installs, a real provider and real disk exhaustion need their own checks. The fixtures say each file's size, so the notice's bytes and Starting are covered by the renderer tests only. Layouts below 960 CSS px are CDP viewport emulation, standing in for a zoomed window. The update notice and the TMDB ring beside the notice are not shown in this scenario; the bar folds by what it measures, so they count as any other width there, and the renderer test dismisses Update with its layout supplied. The arrow tries its words again when the pages' room grows, the words change or the interface language does, measured before the bar is drawn. On Windows, the [installed upgrade check](../../../../docs/contributing/testing.md#installed-upgrade) keeps copies, artwork and progress across two upgrades and plays them with the app and its ffmpeg blocked from the network.

The folded-page check probes 760 down to 520 CSS px in 20 px steps and records `notice.foldedWidth`, requiring the actual folded Downloads control and current-page marker before exercising its pointer/keyboard paths. Header copy changes the width where folding starts; 760 px alone may still fit all page names. The existing 960/760 layout checks remain required separately.
