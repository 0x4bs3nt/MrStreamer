# Live TV

## Sub-features

Pick channels through global search, Live TV, its guide grid or favourites. Watch shares its live stream with the muted Home preview. Other paths include codec conversion, captions, recovery, favourite ordering and catch-up of programmes that aired.

## How to get to it (user POV)

After connecting, **Search** or Ctrl-K/Cmd-K finds channels and programmes. Enter starts the selected channel. **Live TV** provides channel/category and guide browsing. Its category picker shows Favourites, Recently watched and All channels, then one row per country with ungrouped categories in provider order. Enter or a click opens a country; Left or Back returns with that country focused. Find searches country and category names across the catalogue, separately from channel/programme search. The same picker is in Watch's channel overlay. A remembered category opens inside its country; a removed category falls back to All channels with a message. A row's chevron, or Right, opens the rest of its day; on a channel with catch-up it starts with today's programmes that aired and the provider keeps, and a click plays one from its start in Watch, which says "Aired …" under the programme's title. Back in Live TV or Home, the preview goes on with the recording and says the same. Escape leaves Watch for Home; **Watch** returns to the current channel. **Guide**, beside Watch and mute in Live TV's strip, or at the end of the list's title while nothing plays, or G, shows the same list as a grid of channels by time, past included, and **List**, G or Escape goes back. Favourites are saved from channel controls and appear on Home and Live TV. **CC** lists the channel's subtitles and, under them, **Timing** while teletext, captions or HLS text shows, and the look's Size, Background and Position. **More** has no subtitle settings.

A failed channel's message, here or on a TV and in the mini player, closes with its cross (**Close message**). The channel stays failed and the message stays closed while the controls wake; **Watch** or R tries again and the next failure shows its own message. A lost TV's closed message stays closed while receivers are found or the list scans; reaching the TV again and losing it shows it again. R on a lost TV that has gone from the list is refused before it connects, so the TV stays lost and "… didn't answer" shows at the top right for a few seconds, each retry its own and closed with its own cross. Closed from the keyboard, focus moves to Watch, and Enter or Space on the cross is its own rather than the channel list's or a retry's. A TV connect that reached no receiver closes the same way, though its note sits outside the picture area. ## Gotchas

Only the synthetic codec-test channels play media. A final frame can conceal stopped playback; require clock progress and decoded sound. Home deliberately keeps the stream running. Linux Xvfb proves functional decoding only; audible sound, GPU use, real streams and AirPlay/Cast remain separate checks.
