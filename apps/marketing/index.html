Mr. Streamer IPTV without the junk.

Mr. Streamer is a free, open source IPTV player for Mac, Windows and Linux. No ads, no account. Bring the subscription you already have.

What you need

An IPTV subscription. Mr. Streamer includes no channels and sells none. It plays what your provider gives you.

Xtream Codes access gets you live TV, movies and series. That's a server address, username and password, or an M3U link that carries them. A playlist gets live TV, and movies and series once you map its groups.

The guide comes from your provider. Channels it doesn't cover show their name and category instead.

Your login goes to your provider, in every request the app makes to it. Favourites, history and settings stay on your computer. No account required. Basic usage statistics are on by default and can be turned off in Settings.
